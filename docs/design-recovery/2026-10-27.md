## Repository-specific records

### D13 — Comic Pop: sequential panels, print energy, and expressive utility

**Use when:** When developing Comic Pop, sticker/panel components, bold editorial UI, or print-derived visual modes.

[Recovered intent] Comic Pop is the loud graphic sibling in the material family: comic panels, pop-art crops, sticker borders, halftone, action badges, and zine misregistration. The recovered modes are Comic Panel, Pop Art, Sticker, and Zine, not a single indiscriminate collage.

[Design DNA] Let panel sequence establish reading order; speech bubbles attach clearly to their source; outlines define interaction boundaries; halftone and registration shifts remain below functional text. Humor should appear in appropriate copy and feedback, not obscure action labels.

[Observed boundary] The README is a development proposal with example component paths and an instruction to add three sticker files. That instruction does not prove automatic ingestion or safe rendering.

[Repair resolution — proposed] Define mode-specific token/asset contracts, clear focus/disabled/error states, and reduced-motion equivalents. Treat uploaded HTML stickers as content with a rendering boundary, not arbitrary executable code. Separate decorative action words from accessible control names.

[Reimagined horizon] An expressive communication kit for campaigns, educational interactives, editorial stories, and playful products, with exportable stickers and coherent sequential templates.

[Carry-forward test] A three-panel interactive story remains understandable in DOM order, keyboard-only use, monochrome, mobile, and static print.
